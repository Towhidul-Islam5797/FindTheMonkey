GOAL
Play opens a world selection screen instead of loading JungleMap
directly. The player picks a world, then a level, and the level starts
with the normal 3-2-1-GO countdown.

DEVELOPER DECISIONS
- Flow B: world page, then a level page 1-5 inside WorldSelection
  (chosen over flow A after seeing the two pages in the scene).
- Worlds unlock step by step: Jungle open, Beach after Jungle level 5,
  Snow after Beach level 5. Inspector box "Unlock All Worlds" for
  testing, off by default.
- Reuse the map window UI the developer copied into WorldSelection.
  The real MapWindowController is NOT used there (it hides the window on
  Awake and tries to start a level when StartSelectedLevel is 1). A new
  small script drives the screen instead, built like MapWindowController
  (per-world Inspector blocks, buttons found by name, per-world level
  button backgrounds, LOCKED and star labels).
- Level button backgrounds filled like MapWindowController.
- 5 files approved (script, MainMenuController, scene, CLAUDE.md, this
  file), including clearing all 10 leftover OnClick entries and fixing
  the out-of-date CLAUDE.md lines.

FOLLOW-UP FIX (2026-10-25, developer approved, 3 files)
- Found by the developer: locked level and world buttons in
  WorldSelection looked faded and see-through, while locked level
  buttons in the in-game map window look solid. Cause: Unity's default
  Disabled Color is half transparent; MapWindowController forces it
  opaque in PrepareLevelButtonVisual, WorldSelectionController did not.
- WorldSelectionController.cs: new PrepareButtonVisual (same rule:
  Sprite Swap to Color Tint, Disabled Color alpha 1, Image alpha 1),
  called before and after setting interactable on level buttons and on
  world buttons. New GetButtonImage helper, also used by
  ApplyLevelButtonBackgrounds (same behaviour as before).
- CLAUDE.md 2.4e: one line.
- Also found: Level Count had been set to 12 for all three worlds in the
  scene, so Beach waited for Jungle level 12 (does not exist) and stayed
  locked although Jungle_LevelCompleted_5 = 1. The developer sets the
  three Level Counts back to 5 by hand.
- Not reported yet: locked buttons look like the in-game ones; Beach and
  Snow open with Level Count 5.
