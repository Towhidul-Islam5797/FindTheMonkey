Project: Find The Cheeky Monkey (working title: Monkey Rush)

GOAL
Fill the empty Inspector fields on LevelCompletePanelController in
JungleMap and BeachMap, so the panel shows the earned stars, and so the
time-out loss in JungleMap hides the Next button.

PROBLEM (found by reading the scene files)
