Project: Find The Cheeky Monkey (working title: Monkey Rush)

GOAL
Every world (Jungle, Beach, Snow) has 12 playable levels instead of 5.
Levels 6 to 12 get a rising difficulty. Extra hiding objects follow in
Sprint 10b.
