SPRINT 02 - TIME BONUS COINS
Project: Find The Cheeky Monkey (working title: Monkey Rush)
Engine: Unity 6000.3.6f1

GOAL
Pay extra coins when the player finishes a level fast. The bonus uses
the elapsed time from Sprint 01c. Stars, score, bananas and the normal
coin reward are not changed.

DESIGN DECISIONS
- Three time tiers, set in the Inspector:
  within 20 seconds = tier 3, within 40 = tier 2, within 60 = tier 1,
  slower = no bonus.
- Bonus coin totals per tier: tier 1 = 1, tier 2 = 2, tier 3 = 3.
- The bonus is cumulative per level, like the star rewards. The best
  tier is saved per level. Improving the tier pays only the difference.
  Repeating the same tier or a worse one pays 0.
- The bonus reuses MapLevelManager.CalculateCumulativeReward. No new
  reward math.
- The bonus is claimed after the normal coin reward and after
  CompleteCurrentLevel(). The order in section 2.4 of CLAUDE.md is
  unchanged.
- The time never ends a level or blocks a tap (invariant 14).
- Everything is optional. If a scene has no LevelTimeBonus or no
  LevelTimer, no bonus is paid and nothing breaks. If the Time Bonus
  Text is not assigned, the bonus is still paid but not shown.
- New PlayerPrefs key: {map}_LevelBestTimeTier_{n}. No existing key was
  renamed or removed.

CHANGES
- New: Assets/Scripts/LevelTimeBonus.cs (and its .meta file)
  Inspector fields for the tier limits and the bonus totals. Public
  method ClaimBonus(elapsedSeconds) returns the coins to add and saves
  the best tier.
- Edited: Assets/Scripts/LevelCompletePanelController.cs
  New optional field timeBonusText. New private fields levelTimeBonus
  and lastTimeBonus. ResolveReferences() finds LevelTimeBonus.
  ShowPanel() claims the bonus after the coin reward block and adds the
  coins with LevelState.AddCoins. UpdatePanelUI() writes
  "Time bonus: +N".
- No scene or prefab edits by the assistant. No other script changes.
