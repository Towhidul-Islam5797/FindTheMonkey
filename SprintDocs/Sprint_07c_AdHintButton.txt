DESIGN CHANGE IN THIS SPRINT (developer decision after the first test)
- The ad hint button ignores coins: showAdHintOnlyWithoutCoins set to 0
  in all three scenes, default false in LevelHint.cs.
- Separate limits: 1 coin hint (hintsPerLevel) and 1 ad hint (new field
  adHintsPerLevel = 1) per level. A level can have both.
- An ad closed early does not use up the ad hint.
- LevelHint.cs changes: new field adHintsPerLevel and counter
  adHintsUsed (reset with hintsUsed when a new level's Player becomes
  active); the hintsUsed check moved from CanGiveHint to CanUseHint;
  CanUseAdHint checks adHintsUsed; HandleAdFinished counts adHintsUsed;
  new OnValidate refreshes both buttons when an Inspector value changes
  during Play (the live checkbox toggle did nothing before).
- CLAUDE.md 2.4d and smoke test step 17 updated.
- 5 files approved for this change (3 scenes, LevelHint.cs, CLAUDE.md).

INVARIANTS CHECKED (CLAUDE.md section 3)
- 5: no LevelState or MapLevelManager added.
- 9: Canvas UI only.
- LevelHint.cs changed only for the design change above. A hint still
  decides nothing (3) and only works after the monkey stops (2). No
  PlayerPrefs key added (6); adHintsPerLevel has a default (8).
