SPRINT 14 - GENTLER SPEED GROWTH
Project: Find The Cheeky Monkey (working title: Monkey Rush)
Engine: Unity 6000.3.6f1
Branch: TowhidulRahat

GOAL
After Sprint 13 (20 percent slower) the last cycles were still too fast
to follow, because Speed Increase Per Cycle was as large as Start Speed
(the monkey got one full Start Speed faster every cycle). Make the speed
grow gently.

CHANGES (direct scene edits, script speed_increase.ps1 in the session
scratchpad)
Speed Increase Per Cycle = 25 percent of Start Speed on the Player's
CharacterPathMover in Level_1 to Level_12, in JungleMap, BeachMap and
SnowMap. 12 lines per scene, 36 in total, nothing else. Start Speed kept
from Sprint 13. Dry run first; written scenes byte-identical to the dry
run. The three worlds have the same values.

Top speed in the last cycle = Start + Increase x (Cycles - 1), px/s:
Level  Start  Increase before -> after  Cycles  Top before -> after
1      800    800 -> 200                4       3,200 -> 1,400
2      800    800 -> 200                8       6,400 -> 2,200
3      1040   1200 -> 260               12      14,240 -> 3,900
4      1200   1200 -> 300               16      19,200 -> 5,700
5      1600   1600 -> 400               16      25,600 -> 7,600
6      1200   1200 -> 300               12      14,400 -> 4,500
7      1440   1440 -> 360               14      20,160 -> 6,120
8      1600   1600 -> 400               16      25,600 -> 7,600
9      1600   1600 -> 400               14      22,400 -> 6,800
10     1760   1760 -> 440               16      28,160 -> 8,360
11     2000   2000 -> 500               16      32,000 -> 9,500
12     2000   2000 -> 500               18      36,000 -> 10,500

Unchanged: Start Speed, cycles, wait at point (0.2 s), stop points,
randomize, code.

INVARIANTS CHECKED (CLAUDE.md section 3)
- 1, 2, 3: StartGame, the tap gate and right/wrong are untouched.
- 12: the Animator field is untouched.

TRADE-OFF TO WATCH
A slower monkey takes longer to finish its walk. Level 12 has 108 stops
(18 cycles x 6 points); its walk grows from about 23 s to about 30-35 s
(estimate). The level timer starts only when the monkey stops, so time
bonuses are not affected. If waiting feels long, lower Total Cycles in
the long levels as a separate change.

DONE CONDITIONS
- All three scenes open with no red errors.
- The monkey can be followed with the eye in the hard levels (9 to 12)
  and still stops and hides correctly.
- Later levels still feel harder than early ones.
