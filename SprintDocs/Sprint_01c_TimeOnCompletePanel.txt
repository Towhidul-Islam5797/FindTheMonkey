SPRINT 01c - TIME ON LEVEL-COMPLETE PANEL
Project: Find The Cheeky Monkey (working title: Monkey Rush)
Engine: Unity 6000.3.6f1

GOAL
Show the time the player spent on the level on the level-complete
panel, as "Time: M:SS". Rewards do not use the time.

DESIGN DECISIONS
- LevelTimer counts elapsed seconds from the moment the timer starts
  (when the monkey stops moving, see Sprint 01b) until the monkey is
  found.
- Elapsed time keeps counting after the 60 second countdown reaches 0.
  The countdown value stays at 0 because of Mathf.Max. IsRunning now
  stays true after 0 until StopTimer() runs. Nothing else reads
  IsRunning.
- The panel text is optional. If Time Text is not assigned, the time is
  not shown and nothing breaks.
- The reward calculation and its order (coin reward before
  CompleteCurrentLevel) are not changed.

CHANGES
- Edited: Assets/Scripts/LevelTimer.cs
  New private field elapsedSeconds. New property ElapsedWholeSeconds.
  Update() adds Time.deltaTime to elapsedSeconds while running. The two
  lines that set IsRunning to false at 0 were removed. StartTimer()
  resets elapsedSeconds.
- Edited: Assets/Scripts/LevelCompletePanelController.cs
  New optional field timeText. New private field levelTimer, found in
  ResolveReferences(). UpdatePanelUI() writes the time after the tries
  text.
- No scene, prefab, or other script changes.
