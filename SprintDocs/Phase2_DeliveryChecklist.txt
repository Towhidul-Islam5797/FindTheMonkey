4. KNOWN DEBT CARRIED OVER (NOT IN THIS BUILD)
Release blockers (before any store release)
- Real AdMob account, App IDs and ad unit IDs.
- Consent (UMP) flow.
- AD_ID permission: the AdMob SDK adds it; Play's Families policy
  expects child-directed apps not to request it. Remove it in the
  manifest before release.
- Release keystore and signed AAB for Google Play; store listing.
- iOS build (needs a Mac with Xcode and an Apple developer account).
Features and polish
- Settings panel with saved Music, Sound, Vibration, Privacy Policy
  and Rate Us behind a parental gate (Sprint 18, next phase).
- Main Menu, World Selection and Splash backgrounds still stretch on
  non-9:16 screens; level grid not checked at tablet width (left from
  Sprint 16).
- No wrong-tap sound clip. Vibration is one fixed buzz.
- Empty placeholder scenes: LevelSelection, MonkeyCollection, Rewards,
  Settings (disabled in the build).
- Daily rewards not built.
- Interstitial count is per session; no frequency cap across sessions.
- Rewarded ad: reward callback is expected before the closed callback;
  check on device.
- iOS: gameplay pause under the rewarded ad not checked on a device.
Code health
- Debug logging is on in many scripts (showDebugLogs).
- Unused scripts: ArrivalLayerTrigger, LevelProgressionController,
  MapLevelLoader, GameplayInputBlocker, GameplayInputGuard.
- Hard-coded SelectLevel1() to SelectLevel10(); world and level lock
  rules exist in two scripts.
- MapWindowController is about 1,800 lines.
- URP version warning (manifest 17.6.0, installed 17.3.0).
