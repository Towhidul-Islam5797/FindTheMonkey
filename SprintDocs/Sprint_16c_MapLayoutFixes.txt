SPRINT 16C - MAP LAYOUT FIXES (RESPONSIVE UI, STEP 3)
Project: Find The Cheeky Monkey (working title: Monkey Rush)
Engine: Unity 6000.3.6f1
Branch: TowhidulRahat

GOAL
Make the three map scenes look right on screens that are not 9:16,
now that the Canvas Scaler uses Expand (16a).

FINDINGS (from the scene files; no device notes yet)
- Backgrounds (941x1672, 9:16) were stretched to the screen with
  Preserve Aspect off: about 22 percent taller on 19.5:9 phones,
  about 33 percent wider on 3:4 tablets.
- HUD was anchored and pivoted top-left with a fixed width of 979, so
  on wide screens it hugged the left edge.
- grass_r (Beach, Jungle) was anchored bottom-center at x 500, so on
  wide screens it floated in from the right edge.
- In BeachMap and SnowMap the grass was a child of the background, so
  any background resize would also move the grass.

DEVELOPER DECISIONS
- 16c approved for BeachMap, JungleMap, SnowMap (2026-10-28).
- Unity was running; the developer confirmed the scenes were saved.
- Menus and Splash backgrounds moved to 16d.

CHANGES (direct scene edits)
All three map scenes:
- Canvas/HUD RectTransform: anchors and pivot (0, 1) -> (0.5, 1),
  anchored x 0 -> -50.5. Same rect at 1080 wide, centered on wider
  screens. Children and the layout group unchanged.
- Background (beach_gameplay, jungle_gameplay, snow_gameplay): new
  built-in AspectRatioFitter (fileID 258720845), Envelope Parent,
  ratio 0.5628 (941/1672). The art keeps its shape and covers the
  screen; edges are cropped instead of stretched.
BeachMap and SnowMap:
- New Canvas/Grass (GameObject 695162145, RectTransform 695162146),
  stretched, right after the background and before Levels (same draw
  order as before). grass_l, grass_r and grass_m moved into it from
  the background. Same setup as JungleMap's Grass.
BeachMap and JungleMap:
- grass_r anchors (0.5, 0) -> (1, 0), anchored x 500 -> -40. Same spot
  at 1080 wide, stays at the right edge on wider screens.
SnowMap grass anchors unchanged (it sits near the objects, not at the
edges).

INVARIANTS CHECKED (CLAUDE.md section 3)
- 9: Canvas UI only.
- 7, 8: no script or serialized field changed.
- HintButton sibling order (2.4d) unchanged: Grass goes before Levels.
- Gameplay logic, taps and level roots untouched.

KNOWN LIMITATION
Hiding objects are centered; the background art scales a little
around the center on non-9:16 screens, so art near the top or bottom
can shift up to about 130 units relative to the objects (the old
stretch did the same).
